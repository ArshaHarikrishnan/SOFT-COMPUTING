Assessment & Practice Questions

Questions aligned with the CST444 syllabus and model assessment material.

Perceptron – Classification: Find the weights for classification of given patterns using a Perceptron network, assuming suitable learning rate and initial weights.
Adaline: Explain the architecture and training of Adaline. Use Adaline to train a NOR logic function with bipolar inputs and targets and perform two epochs.
Widrow–Hoff / LMS: Explain the Widrow–Hoff learning rule (LMS).
Adaline Problem: Use Adaline to implement an AND function and perform one epoch of training.
Back Propagation: Explain the training process and weight updates in a Back Propagation Network.
Perceptron OR: Perform one epoch of Perceptron training for an OR function and discuss convergence.
Perceptron and XOR: Discuss the convergence theorem for Perceptron learning and the limitation illustrated by the XOR problem.
